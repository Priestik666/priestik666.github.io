COMMON QUESTIONS

Before the rack is opened.

Does the service include network reconfiguration?

No. The base service covers physical organization, labeling and basic documentation. Network redesign, switch configuration and electrical work require a separate scope.

Will the rack need downtime?

Some organization work may require temporary disconnection. The required access and acceptable work window are agreed before the visit.

Are patch cords and materials included?

Basic organization consumables are included only where stated. Replacement patch cords, custom components and the BORIS Rack Rescue Kit are priced separately.

Where is the service available?

The initial offer is designed for on-site work in Almaty. Other locations can be reviewed individually.
